## Headline findings

1. **New/tiny accounts break out constantly.** Examples: @luca_fawcett 276 followers → 523K views (350×); @gxbriel.mntl 3.5K → **11.3M** (3210×); @achievedfreedom 940 → 224K (84×); @siddharthgumber07 178 → 87K (72×). **The niche rewards the content, not the account size** — huge opportunity for a new brand.
2. **The COMEBACK angle is proven and unowned.** Top on-brand outlier: **@cyrusliftss — "I haven't been consistent in the gym… time to lock back in" → 877K (379×).** Also @luca_fawcett "I've been average my whole life" (523K). The audience rewards *fell-off → getting back up* — exactly Run it Back — but no one owns it as a brand identity. **This validates the comeback niche-down.**
3. **The niche is saturated with hype, thin on mechanism.** ~90% is aspiration/vibes (cinematic gym b-roll + motivational text/VO). Almost none teach *why it works* (the science). **Our "Discipline Lab / mechanism" differentiation is an open lane.**
4. **A viral script travels across accounts.** "How can you be the man of her dreams when you're not the man of your own" appeared on **two** different accounts (302 followers→26K and 92.5K→834K) — creators reuse proven audio/scripts. We can ride proven scripts in our own voice.

## Winning formats (ranked by how replicable they are for a faceless/new account)

1. **Gym/cinematic B-roll + static text overlay** (a one-line motivational truth over moody footage). Cheapest, fastest ("within an hour"), and tiny accounts blow up with it. Music-only. *Most replicable.*
2. **B-roll over voiceover** (cinematic montage + motivational VO). Our exact planned build (Higgsfield b-roll + AI/real VO). Aspirational.
3. **Listicle text** ("10 uncomfortable signs you're not living up to your potential"). @_darrenhugo_ hit 343K & 65K on a 2K-follower account with this. Punchy, saveable.
4. **Vulnerable relatable story** (VO or talking head): "I've been average my whole life," "I haven't been consistent." Low-fi, high emotion, massive outliers.
5. **POV relatability**: "POV: you only slept 3 hours but still went to the gym" (11.3M). One-line premise + gym clip.
6. **Talking-head monologue / hard truth** (education): @rayriv5 (sobriety, 63K), @irob.lux (self-discipline, 205K). Needs on-camera confidence — better once we add the character.
7. **Day-in-the-life beat-synced montage**: @thetruejulianmendez (588K), routine porn. Higher effort (multi-location filming).
