# PART 1 — Honest Assessment

## What's genuinely strong (keep)

1. **Name–pain fit is exceptional.** Research showed the niche's deepest wound is the
   restart-shame loop — and "Run it Back" *is* that promise. Validated by live data (comeback
   hooks overperform: 877K/379× outlier) and unowned by anyone. This is real, rare positioning.
2. **The differentiation stack is coherent and layered.** Comeback niche + mechanism/science +
   maker's honest tool reviews + humour + anti-grindset. Each layer reinforces the others, and
   each attacks a specific weakness of the competition (hype without substance, quotes without
   method, humorless grindset, anonymous pages with no authority).
3. **Monetization is mapped before day one** — funnel (reach → email → affiliate → own products),
   product/affiliate landscape researched, FTC guardrails known. Most creators figure this out
   in year two; it's done.
4. **The identity is built.** Visual system, logo, voice, bios, templates, first assets. Nothing
   blocks posting.
5. **The content formula is monetization-native:** *entertain → practical advice → the tool that
   makes it stick.* Value and revenue are the same motion, not competing ones.

## What's weak or risky (fix / watch)

1. **⚠️ Zero posts published. 32 commits of strategy, 0 reps.** The plan is now the risk. Every
   additional strategy doc has diminishing value until content is live and generating data. A
   discipline brand that hasn't shipped is the joke it should be making.
2. **Scope creep is real.** In one week the brand added: designer → maker, physical → +digital,
   health/wealth → +business/build-in-public, serious → +humour. Each addition is individually
   sound, but 6 series × 4 tones × 3 platforms is a *lot* for one person. Untested breadth is a
   liability; the data must narrow it.
3. **Unresolved production questions:** face vs. semi-faceless (testing planned, not started);
   video assembly pipeline unproven (environment blocked auto-render; CapCut manual flow untested);
   founder's available hours/week never stated — the cadence may be unrealistic.
4. **Assumptions not yet earned:** all growth/monetization numbers are research-based, not
   account-based. Affiliate programs not yet joined; email/lead magnet not built; the audience is 0.
5. **Platform risk on day one:** everything rides on short-form algorithms until the email list
   and SEO blog exist. (Known, planned — but currently 100% exposure.)

## Verdict
**The strategy phase is complete — and over-complete.** Grade: strategy A−, execution F (nothing
shipped). The single most valuable thing now is reps: publish, measure, narrow. The plan below is
deliberately gated on *signals, not calendar dates* — but with hard shipping deadlines so the
strategy machine can't keep running instead of the content machine.

**Rule going forward: no new strategy docs until 30 posts are live.** (Meta-note: this plan IS
"never miss twice" applied to the business itself — and that's build-in-public content.)

# PART 2 — The Master Plan (5 phases, gated)

**The through-line:** Reach (short-form) → Trust (credibility + email) → Money layer 1 (affiliate)
→ Money layer 2 (own digital product) → Money layer 3 (community/scale). Each phase has a GOAL,
ACTIONS, a GATE (signal that unlocks the next phase), and a hard deadline for its first action.

## Phase 0 — SHIP (this week; hard deadline: 7 days)
**Goal:** exist in public. **Everything is ready — this is now purely an act of will.**
- Set bios on IG / TikTok / YT / X (`content/bios.md`); simple link hub in bio.
- Post Launch Pack 01 (pin Post 1); repurpose to all platforms.
- Decide the weekly time budget honestly (hours/week) — this calibrates cadence for everything below.
- Record ONE first video (phone + your voice is enough; imperfect on purpose — it's on-brand).
**Gate:** first 5 posts live. *Nothing else in this plan matters until this happens.*

## Phase 1 — FIND THE SIGNAL (weeks 1–6)
**Goal:** content-market fit — discover which 2–3 formats/tones YOU can sustain and the algorithm rewards.
- Cadence: 4–7 posts/week (calibrated to the real time budget). Batch weekly. **Never miss twice.**
- Run the face vs. semi-faceless test (`on-camera-test-plan.md`): ~70% semi base, face on story/Gear.
- Spread bets across the stack: comeback cuts, discipline-lab science, humour skits/roasts,
  a low-key Gear review, one build-in-public post. Track mode + series + hook per post.
- Weekly 30-min review: retention, saves, sends (ignore likes/follower count). Double down on top 2–3, kill the rest.
- Community reps: reply to every comment (first hour especially); turn best comments into posts.
**Gate:** ≥30 posts published AND 2–3 formats that repeatedly out-retain the rest. (Follower count
is NOT the gate — signal is.)
**Explicitly deferred in this phase:** no affiliate links, no selling, no new strategy docs, no website build.

## Phase 2 — OWN THE AUDIENCE (weeks ~4–10, overlaps Phase 1)
**Goal:** convert attention into owned audience + credibility.
- Build the **lead magnet**: "The Reset Protocol" (or similar) — a free, sharp PDF/notion doc:
  the never-miss-twice system + a 7-day resumable reset. On-brand, serves the Restarter.
- Email capture behind it (simple: link-in-bio → landing page → email). Plug in every caption.
- Sign up now (free, no reach requirements): **Amazon Associates**, **Bookshop**; add an Amazon
  storefront ("the honest gear shelf") once eligible (~1k followers). Links go in the hub, NOT pushed in content yet.
- Start the founder's **build-in-public thread** (weekly post: what I tried, what flopped, numbers).
**Gate:** lead magnet live + first 100 email subscribers.

## Phase 3 — MONEY LAYER 1: AFFILIATE (months ~2–5)
**Goal:** first revenue, without spending trust.
**Trigger:** consistent output + email running + ~1–5k followers on the strongest platform.
- Join the researched programs as thresholds unlock: Impact (Oura/Whoop/Therabody), Brick (direct),
  Light Phone (Tapfiliate), HigherDOSE/ShareASale, Refersion (Rocketbook/Intelligent Change);
  **TikTok Shop at 5k**. Verify every live rate before quoting (per `gear-affiliate-map.md`).
- Gear reviews now carry links — **only via the formula** (advice → the tool that makes it stick),
  always FTC-disclosed, always the honest verdict (including "don't buy it" — those build the most trust).
- Email = the conversion layer: monthly "what I actually use" + the affiliate recs to the warm list.
- Start the **SEO blog** on the website (2–4 posts/month: "Brick review from a product designer,"
  "Whoop vs Oura for discipline") — the compounding, algorithm-proof affiliate asset.
**💰 Expectation set honestly:** first dollars month ~2–3 (books/small items, likely <$100/mo).
Meaningful affiliate ($300–$1,000/mo) realistically month ~5–8 with consistent output. High-ticket
(cold plunge/Eight Sleep) and recurring (apps) improve this over time.
**Gate:** first $500/mo affiliate for 2 consecutive months, OR email list ≥1,000 — whichever first.

## Phase 4 — MONEY LAYER 2: OWN PRODUCT (months ~5–9)
**Goal:** first owned revenue — margin and independence from other people's products.
- **Product v1 = digital, cheap, on-pain:** the paid version of the reset — e.g. **"Run It Back:
  the 30-day comeback system"** ($15–$40: the protocol + tracker + the never-miss-twice rules).
  Digital-first (validated by goodmindsquote's model + coaching-economy research); built from
  content that already proved it resonates.
- Launch to the email list first (that's what it was built for), then to the audience.
- Keep affiliate running underneath; the two stack.
**Gate:** first $1k of own-product sales.

## Phase 5 — MONEY LAYER 3: COMPOUND (months ~9–18)
**Goal:** durable business, multiple engines.
- **Community** (Skool — doubles as the 40%-recurring affiliate we mapped): paid "the Comeback Club" —
  accountability + the system + the people. Recurring revenue, deepest moat.
- **YouTube long-form** (the trust + search + ad-revenue engine; Shorts feed it).
- Product ladder: journal/physical product (your industrial-design skills become the product —
  a designed *object* for the comeback system), courses, possibly the Higgsfield character IP.
- Sponsorships only if on-ethos; by now you have leverage to be picky.

## Operating rhythm (from Phase 1 onward)
- **1 batch day/week** (produce), **daily 20 min** (post + reply), **weekly 30 min** (metrics → narrow),
  **monthly 1 hr** (review this plan's gates; update `brand-direction.md` decisions log).

## Kill / adjust criteria (pre-commitment against sunk cost)
- A series that's bottom-quartile retention for 3 straight weeks → kill it, no sentiment.
- If after 60 posts nothing repeatedly outperforms → the *format* is wrong, not the niche: change
  format radically (e.g., go full face, or full humour) before questioning the positioning.
- If a platform gives 3× the traction of others → shift 70% of effort there.

## The money math (sober summary)
| When | Revenue source | Realistic range |
|---|---|---|
| Month 0–2 | none (by design — trust first) | $0 |
| Month 2–4 | first affiliate trickle (books, small gear) | $10–100/mo |
| Month 5–8 | affiliate layer working (gear, apps, high-ticket occasionals) | $300–1,000/mo |
| Month 6–9 | own digital product launches | +$500–2,000/launch, then trailing |
| Month 9–18 | community + product ladder + YT ads | path to $2–10k/mo if audience compounds |

*Condition on all of it: consistent publishing. The single biggest predictor of the entire table
is whether Phase 0–1 actually happens at cadence. Everything downstream is a multiplier on reps.*
