# Growth Phase Plan — 0 → credible following (monetize later)

The plan for the FIRST phase: earn a following and credibility in the niche **before**
monetizing. Affiliate/products come once trust exists. Companion to `content-strategy.md`
and `growth-and-monetization.md`; this one is scoped to "grow-first, no revenue pressure."

_Status: v1 · 2026-07-02 · horizon: first ~90 days_

## Principle: value-first, monetize later
Phase 1 = pure value + audience + email list. No hard selling. Trust is the asset that
*everything* later (affiliate, products) converts on. Selling early spends trust you haven't
earned yet. (This matches the decision to grow before monetizing.)

## The credibility cheat code — put the real designer forward
The brand's unfair advantage: **the founder is a real industrial designer** with genuine
authority. Almost every competitor is an anonymous, interchangeable faceless page that must
*manufacture* authority. So the fastest credibility move is to shift from "faceless" toward
**semi-faceless with a credible human**: show face/hands/voice, "I design physical products
for a living," show the eye and the process.
- Gives **authority** (a designer's verdict > a quote page).
- Gives **differentiation** (stop competing with 10,000 faceless pages).
- Gives a **reason to be on camera** — the trust multiplier that later makes affiliate convert.
- **Decision to confirm:** how far on-camera? Recommended: at least hands + voice + occasional face. The plan works fully-faceless too, just slower to build trust.

## What earns credibility in this niche (the levers)
1. **Authority** — teach the *mechanism* (science, cite research). Be the smart one, not the hype one. (The #1 account, @mindset.therapy at 11M, is education-led.)
2. **Authenticity** — your own comeback story ("I fell off too") beats "grind harder."
3. **Consistency = the credential** — a discipline brand that posts daily *is* the proof. Showing up is the flex.
4. **Utility** — save-able tools/systems, not empty motivation.
5. **Community** — reply to every comment (esp. first hour); turn comments into videos; feature followers.

## Growth game plan (concrete)
1. **Win ONE platform first: TikTok** — best discovery for new accounts (tiny accounts break out; see `../knowledge-base/viral-patterns.md`). Repurpose the same vertical video to Reels + Shorts. Don't spread thin.
2. **Niche hard:** "the comeback + a designer's take on discipline." Specific > broad.
3. **Growth-phase content mix:**
   - ~**60% value/relatable** — Comeback Cuts, Listicles, Discipline Lab science. The reach engine.
   - ~**20% who-you-are** — designer identity, your process, your own comeback. Builds credibility + parasocial trust.
   - ~**20% low-key Gear reviews** — build the review reputation and funnel to email; **no hard sell yet.**
4. **Cadence:** aim 1/day; floor 4–5/week; batch weekly. Model "never miss twice."
5. **Iterate on retention + saves + sends** (NOT likes/followers). After ~20–30 posts, double down on the 2–3 formats/hooks that out-perform; kill the rest.
6. **Hooks:** contrarian / problem-callout / curiosity (from `viral-patterns.md`).
7. **Engagement engine:** first-hour comment replies; reply-videos; DMs.
8. **Email list from day one** — a free lead magnet (e.g. a reset challenge). Owned audience = the real asset, built before there's anything to sell.

## Metrics that matter (this phase)
Watch-through/retention · saves · shares/sends · profile visits · **email signups.**
NOT follower vanity. **Content-market-fit signal:** a few posts consistently out-retain, and followers/email grow steadily.

## Honest timeline
Dozens–hundreds of posts before real traction; most quit at ~20 — the compounding is in the tail.
**90-day goal:** find content-market-fit (2–3 repeatable winning formats), first ~1–5k following,
email list started, credible identity established. **Then** layer monetization.

## First 30 days (focus)
- **Week 1 — Identity:** align bios (comeback + designer) across platforms; set up the lead magnet; post the strongest identity + comeback + science pieces. Introduce yourself (the designer).
- **Week 2 — Pain & reframe:** hit real pains (procrastination, fried focus) with reframes + tools; first low-key Gear piece (Brick — attacks the #1 pain).
- **Week 3 — Double down:** more of whatever retained best in weeks 1–2.
- **Week 4 — Story + proof:** your comeback story + a Gear review; push saves/shares + email signups.
Review retention/share data weekly; let the data pick the winning formats.

## Guardrail
Stay non-toxic / anti-grindset — it differentiates *and* keeps the brand safe for the affiliates/brands you'll want later. Helpful designer > angry alpha.
