# Humour & Entertainment — the attention layer

Entertainment wins attention; the discipline niche is deadly-serious, so humour is **both** a
reach driver **and** a differentiator. This layers on top of everything else — it changes the
*wrapper*, not the substance.

_Status: v1 · 2026-07-02_

## Why it fits Run it Back (not a bolt-on)
- **The niche is humorless** — wall-to-wall grindset + cinematic hype. Humour stands out instantly.
- **It reinforces the anti-grindset positioning** — we're the self-aware ones who don't take the "4am ice-bath guru" act seriously.
- **It fits the voice** — "I've been in the loop too" is already self-deprecating. We laugh *with* the struggle.
- **The data backs it** — relatable hooks over-perform (viral scan: "POV: you only slept 3 hours…" = 11.3M). The comeback is *inherently* funny ("day 1 again, for the 14th time").

## The one principle: entertain → then make it stick
- **Humour/relatability = the HOOK** (earns attention + reach + shares).
- **The real point + tool = the PAYOFF** (earns trust + monetization).
- **Make them laugh, then make it land.** Never a joke with zero substance — that's a meme page with no authority. Entertaining wrapper, real value inside. This plugs straight into the content formula: **entertain → practical advice → the tool that makes it stick.**

## Comedy styles that fit (with example lines)
1. **Relatable / self-deprecating (our secret weapon).** The restart loop is comedy gold.
   - "Day 1 of my discipline era. (14th time this year.)"
   - "Me buying the $200 focus gadget instead of just doing the thing."
   - "My habit tracker has a longer streak of me forgetting to open it."
2. **Satire of the niche** — mock the hype clichés; positions us as the sane alternative.
   - "How discipline influencers think their 5am looks vs. how it actually goes."
   - The shirtless-cold-plunge-guru bit: "I haven't felt joy since 2019 but my HRV is immaculate."
3. **POV skits & recurring characters** — bankable, bingeable.
   - **"Evening You"** sabotaging "Morning You"'s plans (borrowed from Bryan Johnson's "Evening Bryan").
   - The **procrastination gremlin** / the **dopamine goblin** (your phone) whispering at 9pm.
   - "POV: your brain negotiating whether the gym counts if you go tomorrow."
4. **Deadpan gear roasts (the designer's twist — the funniest pillar).** Dry, honest, funny.
   - "It's a $80 bottle that reminds you to drink water. We used to call that… thirst." → then the *honest* verdict.
5. **Meme formats / trending audio** applied to discipline struggles (ride the sound, keep our spin).

## Guardrails
- **Laugh WITH, never punch down.** No manosphere edge, no mocking the viewer — mock the *shared struggle* and the *niche's BS*.
- **Still deliver value.** Entertainment is the wrapper; the substance/tool is why they follow and buy.
- **Don't force it.** A real comeback story can stay 100% sincere — not everything needs a joke.
- **Protect credibility.** The designer/science authority is the trust asset; humour should feel *smart*, not clownish.
