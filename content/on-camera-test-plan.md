# On-Camera Test Plan — Face vs. Semi-Faceless

We won't guess whether to show your face — we'll test it. This lays out a content strategy
for **both** modes and a protocol to let *data + sustainability* decide. Feeds the open decision
in `growth-phase-plan.md`.

_Status: v1 · 2026-07-02_

## The constant (both modes)
- **Your real voice** narrates everything (fixes the VO weakness from Comeback Cut #01 — no more generic TTS).
- **The designer identity** is front-and-center either way ("I design physical products").
- **The only variable = does your face appear.** So this is a clean, low-risk test.

## Approach A — With Face (creator-led)
- **Format:** piece-to-camera / talking head; product reviews in-hand; story-to-camera; "designer reacts."
- **Best series:** ★ The Gear (hold the product, give the verdict), your comeback story, hard-truth hot takes. Face IS the pattern-interrupt hook.
- **Pros:** maximum credibility + parasocial trust; strongest differentiation; best for selling later.
- **Cons:** more exposure/effort; you must be comfortable on camera; harder to batch.
- **Hook style:** your face + a bold spoken claim in the first 2s ("I design products for a living, and this $59 gadget fixed my phone addiction").

## Approach B — Semi-Faceless (hands + voice, no face)
- **Format:** POV / over-the-shoulder; hands showing/using the product; VO over B-roll + your workspace; close-ups; text-on-screen.
- **Best series:** ★ The Gear (hands-on the product), Discipline Lab (VO over B-roll/diagrams/motion graphics), Comeback Cuts, Listicles.
- **Pros:** privacy; lower barrier → easier to keep posting; still credible (real voice + real hands + a designer's desk beats fully faceless); scalable/batchable.
- **Cons:** slightly less parasocial trust than a face.
- **Hook style:** strong visual (hands/product/workspace) + spoken hook; text hook stacked on top.

## Series × mode matrix (how each series runs in each mode)
| Series | With Face | Semi-Faceless |
|---|---|---|
| **The Gear** | you hold it, talk, verdict | hands-on demo + VO ✅ both strong |
| **Discipline Lab** (science) | you explain to camera | VO over B-roll/diagram ✅ easier |
| **Comeback Cut** | you deliver the wake-up | VO over cinematic B-roll (as built) |
| **Never Miss Twice / Listicle** | optional face intro | VO + text + B-roll ✅ |
| **Who-you-are / story** | ★ face is strongest | VO over your workspace/process + photos |

Takeaway: most series work **either** way; **face pays off most** on *story* and *Gear reviews* (trust-heavy). Semi-faceless is the easier default for the reach content.

## The test protocol
**Start semi-faceless as the base** (lower barrier = you keep posting = you get data), and
**deliberately test face on the trust-heavy content** where it should matter most:
1. **Weeks 1–4:** post ~daily. Make **~70% semi-faceless** (Comeback Cuts, Listicles, Discipline Lab) and **~30% with-face** — specifically your **story** pieces and **1–2 Gear reviews**. That's testing face exactly where the theory says it wins.
2. **Label every post** with its mode (a simple tag in a tracking sheet / the post log).
3. **After ~4 weeks / ~20–30 posts, compare by mode:** average **retention**, **saves**, **sends/shares**, and follower/email lift. (Ignore likes.)
4. **Decide on two axes:**
   - **Performance** — which mode out-retains and gets sent more?
   - **Sustainability** — which can you actually produce consistently without dreading it?
   - If face wins performance *and* you can sustain it → go face. If semi performs comparably and is far more sustainable → semi (with face reserved for occasional story/Gear).
5. **Don't over-read early virality** — one viral post isn't the verdict; look at the pattern across the batch.

## Why start this way (not a rigid 50/50 A/B)
A brand-new account has low, noisy volume — a clean 50/50 A/B would just measure algorithm variance. Testing **face where it should matter most** (story + reviews) against a **semi-faceless base** gives a clearer, cheaper read *and* keeps you posting. You're not choosing blind — you're pressure-testing the highest-value hypothesis.

## Decision & next step
Log the call in `growth-phase-plan.md` once the 4-week data is in. Until then, both modes use
your voice + the designer identity, so nothing is wasted either way.
