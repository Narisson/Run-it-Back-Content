# The Gear — Product & Affiliate Map (designer-review angle)

The affiliate engine for Run it Back's **"The Gear"** pillar: a product designer reviews the
physical tools of discipline, focus, and optimization. Built from research (2026-07-02).

> ⚠️ **Every commission rate/cookie below is directional and mostly UNVERIFIED — confirm on
> each brand's own affiliate dashboard before quoting a number on camera.** And note:
> **referral ≠ affiliate** — Whoop/Oura/Eight Sleep public links are *referral* (free month /
> gift card / credit), NOT cash. Apply to their separate cash affiliate (usually via Impact).

## Scope note: physical AND digital tools
"The Gear" covers **both** physical products (below) *and* **digital productivity tools** — focus/
habit/sleep apps, productivity software (e.g. Notion, Todoist, Sunsama, Brain.fm, meditation/sleep
apps), and systems. Digital tools often pay **recurring subscription affiliate** (better long-term
economics than one-off physical sales) — see the app affiliate notes in `growth-and-monetization.md`.
A dedicated digital-tools affiliate pass is a good next research step. The physical map below stands;
digital reviews are equally on-brand and often more monetizable.

## The core strategy: two lists, played differently

1. **Design-icons** (Brick, Light Phone, Daylight, Ugmonk Analog) — the most *review-worthy*,
   on-brand, differentiating products… but mostly **referral-only / weak affiliate.** Use these
   for **content + trust + differentiation** (they're what make you *the designer channel*),
   regardless of payout.
2. **Money-makers** (Oura, Whoop, cold plunge, Eight Sleep, reMarkable) — real cash affiliate
   programs, higher ticket. Use these for **revenue.**

**High-ticket = the money.** A modest % on a $2,000 cold plunge or Eight Sleep beats ~3% on a
$40 gadget all day. Prioritize confirming the big-ticket direct programs.

## Recommended starting lineup

**Flagship first review → Brick.** It's the perfect *content* piece: iconic single-purpose design + it attacks the #1 pain (fried focus/dopamine) we found in the audience research. Even if the affiliate is weak, it *establishes the channel* as the designer's-eye gear reviews. Funnel viewers to the email list; monetize elsewhere.

**Set up these affiliates first (real cash + fit):**
1. **Impact** → Oura, Whoop, Therabody, likely reMarkable/Hatch.
2. **Cold plunge** (Plunge / Ice Barrel) direct — high ticket.
3. **Eight Sleep** media affiliate — high ticket.
4. **Awin** (Ultrahuman), **CJ** (Withings), **ShareASale** (HigherDOSE, Intelligent Change), **Refersion** (Ugmonk, Grovemade, Rocketbook), **Amazon Associates** (everything cheap), **AvantLink** (Rogue).

**Design-cred reviews (brand fit > payout):** Ugmonk Analog, Light Phone, Daylight, Grovemade.

## Content ↔ pain mapping (why each review belongs on Run it Back)
- **Brick / Light Phone / Daylight** → *fried focus / dopamine* (the #1 pain). "The hardware that fixes your attention."
- **Oura / Whoop** → *"measure your discipline"* — data makes consistency visible. Ties to the science pillar.
- **Cold plunge / sauna** → *do hard things* (aMCC, voluntary discomfort). The comeback/discipline flagship.
- **Eight Sleep / Hatch** → *sleep is the foundation* (win the night → win the day).
- **Ugmonk Analog / journals / Time Timer** → *systems over willpower* — the physical system that runs your day.

## Guardrails
- **Confirm every rate** on the brand's affiliate dashboard before saying it on camera.
- **Use the cash affiliate link**, not the referral link, for Whoop/Oura/Eight Sleep.
- **FTC disclosure** on every review (spoken + captioned; "I earn a commission").
- **Only review what passes the ethos test** and, ideally, **what you actually use** — a designer's honest verdict (incl. "don't buy this") is the whole trust asset.
