# Run it Back — Content Strategy & Launch Plan

The plan for what we post to get the brand going. Built on the knowledge base
(`../knowledge-base/`). Audience: **male-discipline core** (the Restarter + Optimizer),
health-led crossover as a later expansion.

_Status: v1 launch plan · 2026-07-02_

## Positioning recap (what every post must serve)
- **Spine:** the inner game — discipline, resilience, mental strength, willpower.
- **Promise:** *the inner game of coming back.* Whatever happened yesterday, you run it back.
- **Proof-grounds:** health and wealth are the visible scoreboards.
- **Core pain we solve:** the restart-shame loop — starting, quitting, restarting from day one.
- **Voice:** honest, non-shaming, "I've been in the loop too," system-first. Demanding *and* inviting (anti-grindset). Never scold.

## How we stand out (differentiation — this is the whole game)

The niche is flooded with two losing formulas: **borrowed hype over cinematic B-roll**
(shadowgrindx_) and **recycled quotes + gossip bait** (goodmindsquote). Run it Back wins by
being the opposite on three axes:

1. **Mechanism, not just motivation.** Every post teaches *why it works* (the brain science)
   and *how to do it* (a tool) — not just "stay hard." We're the discipline brand that
   actually explains the machine. This is our moat: **science + story.** Nobody in the
   quote-page lane does this well.
2. **Resumable discipline, not perfectionist grindset.** We own the *comeback*, not the
   flawless streak. "You're allowed to miss. You're not allowed to quit. Never miss twice."
   This is the emotional counter-position to shame-based hustle culture — and it's literally
   the brand name.
3. **Signature franchises, not random posts.** Recurring named series people follow and
   identify with (below). Series build retention and a recognizable identity; one-off quotes don't.
4. **(Coming) a recognizable character voice** — the semi-faceless Higgsfield persona will
   give us a consistent on-screen "coach," differentiating from the sea of faceless sameness.
   Parked until the plan firms; content starts faceless (VO + visuals + text) and the character
   layers in.

**Verbal signature / sign-off:** end pieces with **"Run it back."** — a repeatable tag that
doubles as the CTA and trains brand recall.

5. **Humour / entertainment** — the niche is humorless; being *funny about the struggle* (self-aware, anti-grindset, never punching down) wins attention AND differentiates. **Entertain → then make it stick.** See `humour-and-entertainment.md`.

## The content formula (every post)
> **Practical advice → the tool that makes it stick.**

Actionable, not just inspirational. Each piece gives a do-it-today takeaway (system/rep/reframe),
and — when it fits — points to the tool (physical or digital) that makes following through easier.
This couples value + tools into one honest move (only recommend what enables the advice) and is
the trust-safe affiliate integration. Not every post sells; every post *helps*.

## Content pillars (spine → proof-grounds)
Rough target mix per week:
- **Discipline strategies** (~30%) — systems, habits, routines, consistency mechanics.
- **Resilience / comebacks** (~25%) — bouncing back, setbacks, the "run it back" spirit.
- **Mental strength** (~20%) — focus, dopamine/attention, emotional control, stress.
- **Willpower / do-hard-things** (~15%) — the aMCC, micro-sucks, voluntary discomfort.
- **Proof-grounds** (~10%) — discipline applied to health & wealth (woven into the above too).

## Signature series (the franchises)
Rotate these so the feed is recognizable, not random:

1. **The Discipline Lab** — 30–45s science breakdowns ("there's a part of your brain that grows when you do hard things"). *Mechanism.* Our differentiator in a series.
2. **Never Miss Twice** — short setback-recovery lessons. The restart-shame reframe. *Core brand.*
3. **Run It Back** — 60s comeback stories (athletes, founders, anyone who fell and returned). *Literal brand name as a series; emotional/shareable.* (Verify all facts before posting.)
4. **Today's Rep** — one small hard thing + the reason it works ("your daily micro-suck"). *Actionable, daily-friendly.*
5. **Proof** — discipline applied to the body or the bank ("comfort is aging you"; "boring compounds"). *Proof-grounds.*
6. **★ The Gear** — a product designer reviews the physical tools of discipline/focus/optimization (Brick, Whoop, Oura, cold plunge…). Build-quality + UX + "does it actually work." *The affiliate engine + sharpest differentiator.* See `gear-affiliate-map.md`. Best in the founder's own voice, hands-on/on-camera.

## Format & production (starting now)
- **Primary format:** vertical short-form video, 20–45s. Structure: **hook (0–2s) → tension/why → tool/payoff → sign-off + CTA.**
- **Faceless build (v1):** AI voiceover + generated/stock B-roll + bold on-screen text + trending-adjacent audio. We have Higgsfield for image/video/audio + voice generation — use it to produce B-roll and VO.
- **Signature look:** lock ONE system — palette, one bold display font, consistent captions style, consistent sign-off card. (To be defined with the `run-it-back-brand` skill.)
- **Character:** add the Higgsfield persona once designed; keep everything else constant so the swap is seamless.

## Platforms & cadence (don't spread thin)
Centering the male core → prioritize where he is:
- **TikTok + Instagram Reels + YouTube Shorts** — same vertical video repurposed to all three. **Primary.**
- **X** — 1 written post/day (thesis/tips); best channel for the male discipline/wealth core + newsletter growth.
- **YouTube long-form** — later, once short-form finds traction (the trust + monetization hub).

**Launch cadence (30-day sprint):** 1 vertical video/day (repurposed x3) + 1 X post/day.
Batch-produce weekly (record/generate 7 at a time) — model the consistency we preach.
If daily isn't sustainable, floor is 4–5/week, but **never miss twice.**

## The funnel (from day one)
Short-form reach → **link in bio → free lead magnet → email "Club"** → (later) low-ticket
product + affiliate. Every profile bio points to the lead magnet. Proposed first lead magnet:
a **resumable discipline/reset challenge** (serves the Restarter, on-brand, low-friction).
Email is the ownership layer — build it from post #1, not "once we're big."

## CTA ladder (what each post asks for)
- Early (0–1k followers): **follow + save** ("save this for your next reset").
- Always: the **"Run it back." sign-off** for recall.
- Once the lead magnet exists: **"free reset challenge in bio."**

## 30-day sprint outline
- **Week 1 — Foundation & identity:** post the strongest signature-series pieces to establish what the brand *is* (what we stand for, "never miss twice," "discipline is a muscle"). Set up bio + lead-magnet capture.
- **Week 2 — Pain & reframe:** hit the audience's real pains (procrastination, fried focus, the rut) with reframes + tools. This is where the Restarter feels seen.
- **Week 3 — Proof-grounds:** discipline applied to health and wealth; show the scoreboards.
- **Week 4 — Depth & comebacks:** Run It Back stories + Discipline Lab science; push saves/shares and email signups.
- Review analytics weekly; double down on the series/hooks that pop.

## Success signals (first 30–60 days)
Not follower vanity — watch: **watch-through / retention**, **saves & shares** (the currency of
this niche), **profile visits → link clicks**, and **email signups.** Iterate hooks toward what retains.
