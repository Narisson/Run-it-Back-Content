# Launch Batch 01 — Ready-to-Shoot Scripts

12 short-form scripts (20–45s) for the first two weeks. Each: **series · pillar · target
persona**, the **hook** (first 1–2s — most important), **VO script**, **on-screen text**,
**visual direction**, **CTA**, and **source/verify** notes.

Production: AI voiceover + generated/stock B-roll + bold captions (Higgsfield for B-roll/VO).
End every piece on the sign-off card: **"Run it back."**

> **Verify before posting:** anything marked ⚠️ — confirm the stat/fact against the primary
> source (see `../../knowledge-base/`). When in doubt, state the concept, not a shaky number.

---

## 1. "Discipline is a muscle — literally"
- **Series:** The Discipline Lab · **Pillar:** Willpower · **Persona:** Restarter/Optimizer
- **Hook:** "There's a part of your brain that physically grows when you do things you don't want to do."
- **VO:** "It's called the anterior mid-cingulate cortex. Studies show it's bigger in people who consistently push through resistance — and it shrinks when you always take the easy road. Discipline isn't a personality you're born with. It's tissue you build. Every hard thing you didn't want to do? That was a rep. So the next time you don't feel like it — that's not a reason to stop. That's the gym opening."
- **On-screen text:** "your willpower has an address 🧠" → "aMCC" → "it GROWS when you do hard things" → "discipline = reps, not personality"
- **Visual:** brain graphic → someone hesitating at a cold shower / 5am alarm → doing it anyway.
- **CTA:** "Save this for the next time you don't feel like it. Run it back."
- **Source/verify:** aMCC concept solid (Huberman KB). ⚠️ Keep it "studies show," don't invent a number.

## 2. "Never miss twice"
- **Series:** Never Miss Twice · **Pillar:** Resilience/Discipline · **Persona:** Restarter (core)
- **Hook:** "You're allowed to miss. You're just not allowed to miss twice."
- **VO:** "Missing one day is an accident. Missing two in a row is the start of a new habit — the wrong one. The people who look disciplined aren't the ones who never fall off. They're the ones who never let one miss become two. So you skipped today? Fine. It's already gone. The only rule that matters: don't miss tomorrow. That's the whole game."
- **On-screen text:** "miss once = accident" → "miss twice = new habit" → "the rule: NEVER MISS TWICE"
- **Visual:** calendar with one X, then a checkmark the next day; not a full relapse.
- **CTA:** "Screenshot this rule. Run it back."
- **Source:** James Clear / Chris Williamson (DOAC KB).

## 3. "Motivation is lying to you"
- **Series:** — · **Pillar:** Discipline · **Persona:** Restarter/Optimizer
- **Hook:** "Motivation is a mood. And you're building your life on a mood."
- **VO:** "Here's the trap: you wait to feel ready. But motivation shows up *after* you start, not before. The most successful people aren't more motivated than you — they just stopped needing to feel like it. They built systems that run when the feeling's gone. Motivation is a feeling. Discipline is a decision you already made. Decide once. Then just execute."
- **On-screen text:** "motivation = a feeling" → "discipline = a decision" → "motivation comes AFTER you start"
- **Visual:** someone lying in bed waiting vs. someone just putting shoes on and going.
- **CTA:** "Which one were you today? Run it back."
- **Source:** Bartlett "consistency over intensity"; Huberman "motivation follows action" (KBs).

## 4. "Your daily micro-suck"
- **Series:** Today's Rep · **Pillar:** Willpower/Health · **Persona:** Restarter
- **Hook:** "Do one thing you don't want to do today. On purpose."
- **VO:** "Not because it's productive. Because it's training. Cold shower. The set you want to skip. The call you're avoiding. Every time you choose the harder path when it doesn't even matter, you're teaching your brain that you're the kind of person who does hard things. Then when it *does* matter — you've already practiced. One micro-suck a day. That's the rep."
- **On-screen text:** "today's rep:" → "1 thing you don't want to do" → "on purpose" → "you're training the response"
- **Visual:** cold water, cold plunge, last rep, made bed — quick cuts.
- **CTA:** "Comment your micro-suck for today. Run it back."
- **Source:** Huberman aMCC/"micro-sucks" (KB).

## 5. "Procrastination isn't laziness"
- **Series:** The Discipline Lab · **Pillar:** Mental strength · **Persona:** Restarter (on-pain)
- **Hook:** "You're not lazy. You're scared. And it's making you feel like a failure."
- **VO:** "Procrastination isn't a time-management problem — it's an emotion problem. You avoid the task because it threatens something: fear of failing, of it not being perfect, of finding out you're not good enough. The task isn't hard. The *feeling* is. So stop trying to force willpower. Name the feeling — 'I'm avoiding this because I'm scared it won't be good.' Then do it badly for ten minutes. Motion kills the fear."
- **On-screen text:** "procrastination ≠ laziness" → "it's fear" → "name it → do it badly for 10 min"
- **Visual:** someone staring at laptop, then just starting messily.
- **CTA:** "Tag someone who needs to hear this. Run it back."
- **Source:** Mark Manson / DOAC 10-minute rule (Audience + DOAC KBs).

## 6. "Cheap dopamine is bankrupting you"
- **Series:** The Discipline Lab · **Pillar:** Mental strength/Focus · **Persona:** Restarter (fried focus)
- **Hook:** "Your phone isn't wasting your time. It's stealing your ability to try hard."
- **VO:** "Every cheap hit — the scroll, the notification, the endless feed — spikes your dopamine, then drops it *below* where you started. Do that all day and your baseline craters. Suddenly real life feels boring and hard things feel impossible. It's not that you're weak. Your reward system is fried. The fix isn't more willpower — it's fewer cheap hits, so effort feels good again. Protect your dopamine like it's money. Because it is."
- **On-screen text:** "cheap hit → spike → CRASH below baseline" → "real life starts feeling boring" → "protect your dopamine"
- **Visual:** doomscroll → grey/flat world → phone down → color returns.
- **CTA:** "Save this. Put the phone down. Run it back."
- **Source:** Huberman dopamine baseline/wave pool (KB). ⚠️ Concept, no invented %.

## 7. "Discipline equals freedom"
- **Series:** — · **Pillar:** Discipline/Resilience · **Persona:** Optimizer/Restarter
- **Hook:** "The most rebellious thing you can do is keep a promise to yourself."
- **VO:** "Everyone thinks discipline is a cage. It's the opposite. The guy who trains, sleeps, and keeps his word has options. The guy who does whatever he feels like is a slave to every craving that walks by. Discipline now buys freedom later. Every small promise you keep to yourself is a deposit. Every one you break is a withdrawal. What's your balance?"
- **On-screen text:** "discipline = freedom" → "keep the promise = deposit" → "break it = withdrawal"
- **Visual:** disciplined morning vs. chaotic scramble; split screen.
- **CTA:** "What promise are you keeping tomorrow? Run it back."
- **Source:** Jocko Willink (DOAC KB).

## 8. "Comfort is aging you" (Proof: Health)
- **Series:** Proof · **Pillar:** Health proof-ground · **Persona:** Restarter/Crossover
- **Hook:** "Comfort feels safe. It's literally aging you faster."
- **VO:** "Your body only runs its repair machinery when it senses a challenge — hard effort, a bit of hunger, cold. Constant comfort tells it there's nothing to adapt to, so it stops upgrading. That's why the people who chase easy get soft, and the ones who seek a little hardship stay sharp. You don't need to suffer. You need to stop avoiding every hard thing. Discipline isn't punishment — it's maintenance."
- **On-screen text:** "comfort → no signal to adapt" → "challenge → repair turns ON" → "hard thing = maintenance"
- **Visual:** couch/takeout vs. training/cold/walk.
- **CTA:** "Go earn some discomfort today. Run it back."
- **Source:** David Sinclair on DOAC (DOAC KB). ⚠️ Frame as his thesis; avoid hard medical claims.

## 9. "Boring compounds" (Proof: Wealth)
- **Series:** Proof · **Pillar:** Wealth proof-ground · **Persona:** Optimizer/Restarter
- **Hook:** "The most boring path to wealth beats every exciting one."
- **VO:** "Nobody makes a viral video about spending less than you earn and investing the rest for twenty years. It's boring. It also works almost every time — while the exciting bets blow up. Same rule as the gym: unglamorous inputs, repeated without interruption, compound into something absurd. Discipline with your money is just discipline with a different scoreboard. Boring compounds. Exciting bankrupts."
- **On-screen text:** "boring compounds" → "exciting bankrupts" → "money = discipline, different scoreboard"
- **Visual:** slow-growing chart vs. hype-crash; steady deposits.
- **CTA:** "Boring wins. Run it back."
- **Source:** JL Collins / Morgan Housel (DOAC KB). ⚠️ Not financial advice — principle only.

## 10. "Win the first hour"
- **Series:** Today's Rep · **Pillar:** Discipline/Health · **Persona:** Restarter
- **Hook:** "How you spend the first hour decides the other 23."
- **VO:** "Reach for your phone first thing and you've handed your focus to strangers before you've had a thought of your own. Flip it. Get light in your eyes. Move your body. Do one hard thing before the world starts pulling. You're not trying to have a perfect morning — you're trying to win the first decision. Win the first hour, and momentum does the rest."
- **On-screen text:** "first hour = the whole day" → "light. move. one hard thing." → "win the first decision"
- **Visual:** phone-in-bed (bad) vs. sunlight + movement (good).
- **CTA:** "What's your first move tomorrow? Run it back."
- **Source:** Huberman morning routine (KB). Keep tools simple (light, movement).

## 11. "It's not the knockdown"
- **Series:** Run It Back · **Pillar:** Resilience · **Persona:** Stuck Drifter/Restarter
- **Hook:** "Nobody's judged by how they got knocked down. Only by whether they got up."
- **VO:** "Everyone gets dropped. The plan fails, the streak breaks, the year goes sideways. That's not the story. The story is the count — the seconds on the floor deciding who you're going to be. Staying down is comfortable. Getting up is a choice you make with a body that's screaming to quit. You don't get judged by the fall. You get judged by the rep after it. So get up. Run it back."
- **On-screen text:** "everyone gets knocked down" → "the story is the count" → "get up"
- **Visual:** boxing-style get-up; sunrise; lacing shoes after a bad day.
- **CTA:** "Send this to someone who got knocked down. Run it back."
- **Source:** Eubank Jr framing (DOAC KB) — principle piece, no specific claims.

## 12. "Systems over goals"
- **Series:** The Discipline Lab · **Pillar:** Discipline · **Persona:** Optimizer/Restarter
- **Hook:** "You don't rise to your goals. You fall to your systems."
- **VO:** "Everyone has the same goal — get fit, get rich, get disciplined. Goals are common. So they can't be why some people win. The difference is the system: the boring, repeatable thing you do daily whether you feel like it or not. A goal is a direction. A system is a vehicle. You can want to be somewhere all day — without a vehicle you're not moving. Stop setting bigger goals. Build a smaller system you'll actually run."
- **On-screen text:** "goals are common" → "systems are rare" → "goal = direction / system = vehicle"
- **Visual:** vision board vs. someone quietly doing the daily rep.
- **CTA:** "Build the system. Run it back."
- **Source:** James Clear (DOAC KB).

## Notes for production
- **Hook first.** If the first line doesn't stop the scroll, nothing else matters — front-load the surprising claim.
- **One idea per video.** Don't teach two things.
- **Captions always** (most watch muted).
- **Repurpose** each to TikTok / Reels / Shorts; adapt #3, #7, #9, #12 into X posts (they're text-strong).
- **Proof the on-screen text** before publishing — muted viewers read the captions, so typos read as low-effort.
