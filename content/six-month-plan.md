# Run it Back — 6-Month Plan (target: 200k stretch)

Operational calendar fitted to real capacity: **2–3 hrs weekday evenings + 5–7 hrs weekends
(~15–22 hrs/week).** Executes `rib-growth` + `MASTER-PLAN.md` phases.

## Honesty header
200k in 6 months = top-percentile outcome (median consistent creator: 10–40k). This niche is
spike-driven — followers arrive in viral bursts, not linearly. **Inputs are the commitment;
follower numbers are ranges.** Miss an input milestone → fix the system. Miss a follower range →
iterate format, don't quit. Never miss two weeks.

## Monthly milestones

| Month | Theme | INPUT milestones (non-negotiable) | Followers base → stretch | Money |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | SHIP & signal | 20–25 posts live · bios + link hub up · lead magnet drafted · face/semi test run · all comments replied | 500–2k → 5k | $0 (by design) |
| **2** | Find the formula | 45–50 total posts · weekly reviews done ×4 · 2–3 formats identified · lead magnet LIVE · first 100 emails · first reply-videos | 2–5k → 15k | $0 |
| **3** | Double down | 70–80 total · 70% of output = proven formats · 10 strategic comments/day habit · Amazon Associates live · first build-in-public monthly recap | 5–15k → 40k | first trickle (<$100/mo) |
| **4** | Scale the winner | 100–110 total · batch system ≤4h for 6 posts · 300+ emails · first collab/duet · TikTok Shop if ≥5k | 15–40k → 80k | $50–200/mo affiliate |
| **5** | Widen the funnel | 130–140 total · YouTube long-form #1–2 · 2–3 collabs · SEO blog post #1–2 · gear reviews carrying links (FTC-clean) | 30–80k → 140k | $100–500/mo |
| **6** | Compound & community | 160+ total · community ritual live (weekly question / comeback features) · 1,000+ emails · Reset Protocol v2 · Phase-4 product decision | 50–100k → **200k stretch** | $300–1k/mo + product greenlight |

Cumulative post floor: **~160 posts in 26 weeks (6–7/wk).** That volume is what makes the stretch
case *possible*; no volume, no lottery tickets.

## The standard week (repeat 26×)

**Every day, inside the evening block (~40 min, non-negotiable core):**
post 1 piece → reply to every comment (first hour esp.) → 10 strategic comments on big niche accounts → log 1 idea from comments.

| Day | Time | Block |
|---|---|---|
| **Mon** (2–3h) | 40m core + | **Review & plan:** 30m metrics review (completion/saves/sends per post → pick week's doubling-down format) · write 3 hooks + 1 script |
| **Tue** (2–3h) | 40m core + | **Write:** 3–4 scripts from this week's angle (hooks first, VO lines second) · 2 X posts drafted |
| **Wed** (2–3h) | 40m core + | **Visuals:** generate/collect B-roll (Higgsfield/vidiQ/phone) for the batch · brand cards as needed |
| **Thu** (2–3h) | 40m core + | **Edit I:** cut 2–3 videos (CapCut: cuts on beat, captions, sign-off card) |
| **Fri** (2–3h) | 40m core + | **Edit II + social:** finish 1–2 edits · build-in-public X thread · schedule weekend posts |
| **Sat** (3–4h) | — | **PRODUCTION BLOCK:** shoot everything at the desk set (VO recording batch, hands/props shots, face tests) — a full week of raw material in one session |
| **Sun** (2–3h) | 40m core + | **Assemble & load:** final edits · queue Mon–Wed posts · 15m week plan · CLOSE THE LAPTOP |

Weekly output: **6–7 posts** (each cross-posted TikTok/Reels/Shorts + 3–5 X posts).

## What changes by month (same week skeleton, evolving payload)
- **M1:** payload = launch pack + intro video + first batches across all 6 series (spread bets). Face vs semi test per `on-camera-test-plan.md`.
- **M2:** payload narrows to best performers; Wed adds lead-magnet build; Fri adds email setup.
- **M3:** Wed adds affiliate signups (Amazon/Bookshop, program applications); reply-videos become a weekly slot.
- **M4:** Sat block gets faster (template reuse) → add 7th post or 1 experiment/wk (new hook style, trend ride).
- **M5:** Sat alternates: one weekend = shorts batch, next = 1 YouTube long-form + shorts. Wed adds 1 SEO blog draft/2wks.
- **M6:** Fri adds community ritual + email weekly send; Sun adds product-decision prep (Phase 4 gate).

## Rules (the schedule's never-miss-twice)
1. A missed evening = fine. **Two consecutive missed evenings = do the 40m core only, skip the block — never zero.**
2. A missed Saturday = Sunday becomes production. Never two missed weekends.
3. If a week collapses: the floor is **4 posts + daily replies.** Below floor twice in a row → cut scope (drop X, drop a series), never cut the core.
4. Protect Mon review — flying blind for a month is how 160 posts get wasted.
5. Sunday close: stop. Burnout is the #1 killer of the entire plan.

## Weekly review card (Mon, 30 min — answer in the log)
1. Which post had best completion? Best sends? Why (hook/format/topic)?
2. What's the ONE format to double down on this week?
3. What gets killed?
4. Email count vs last week? 5. Am I on input-milestone pace for the month?
