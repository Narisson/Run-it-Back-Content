# Intro Video 01 — "This is my 14th Day One" (storyboard & shot list)

The brand-intro video, built to work as a standalone Run it Back piece (show, don't announce).
Direction A: confession hook → funny escalation → the turn → what this is → the rule → sign-off.
**~27s · 1080×1920 · one location · phone + a lamp · shootable in one afternoon.**

## The set: the designer's desk
The channel's visual home: dark desk surface, one warm lamp, notebook, good pen, a gadget or two,
sketch tools. Semi-faceless friendly, cheap, repeatable — and unmistakably *a maker's* space.
Establish it here; reuse it in every future desk video.

## Shot list

| # | Time | Shot | VO / caption | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0–2s | **Top-down: notebook.** Hand flips pages: "DAY 1" crossed out, "DAY 1" crossed out, again… lands on fresh "DAY 1" | "This is my 14th Day One." | The joke is visual; VO confirms. Write pages in advance. (Face variant: 1s deadpan face first.) |

## Audio
- Beats 1–2: light lo-fi/upbeat (slightly too cheerful = part of the joke).
- Beat 3: music CUTS. Silence → lamp **click** → VO alone.
- Beats 4–6: low pulse, builds gently. VO: deadpan front half, warmer + firmer after the turn.
- **Voice: the founder's real voice throughout.**

## Captions
Burned-in the whole way (most watch muted). Brand style: Bone `#F4F7FB` on dark; the rule line
("never miss twice") in Blaze `#FF4A1C`; science line accented Signal blue `#3B82F6`. Mono-font
era labels in beat 2 (`the app era` · `the gym era` · `the 5am era`).

## Assets in this folder
- `end-card.png` — final sign-off card (done).
- `streak-reference.png` — streak motif reference / backup insert (done).
- VO script = the storyboard lines above, verbatim.

## Shooting checklist (one afternoon)
1. Prep notebook pages ("DAY 1" ×5, crossed out; weekly grid page; brain sketch penciled to ink over).
2. Phone on tripod/book-stack: top-down desk position + door position + bedside position.
3. Shoot beats 1, 4a–c, 5 at the desk · 2b at the door · 2c bedside · 3 in the dark.
4. Record VO separately on the phone in a quiet room (blanket fort = free vocal booth) — flat/deadpan first half, warm second.
5. Assemble in CapCut: cuts on the beat, music drop at 7s, captions, end card. Export 1080×1920.
