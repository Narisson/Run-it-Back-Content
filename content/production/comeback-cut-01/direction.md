# Comeback Cut #01 — Direction & Treatment (v3)

_2026-07-18 · supersedes the visual plan in `edit-sheet.md` (v1) and `script-v2.md`; the v2
script wording and timing carry forward. Timeline pressure is off — this is the flagship, we
get it right._

## The job of this video

It's the first thing the brand ever says in motion. It must do three things at once:
1. **Stop the scroll** — proven beat structure (Template A: excuse → drop → wake-up → rule).
2. **Install the flagship idea** — never miss twice. One idea, nothing else.
3. **Look like nobody else** — the screenshot test: if a random faceless motivation page could
   have posted this frame, the frame is wrong.

v1 passed 1 and 2 but failed 3 (stock battle ropes = the exact aesthetic we position against).

## Direction decision: shoot it in the desk world

The intro video already built our ownable visual world: **dark walnut desk, black notebook,
warm lamp from the left, deep navy shadows, 35mm grain, hands only.** Comeback Cut #01 moves
into that same world. Two brand videos, one recognizable universe — consistency compounds into
identity.

**The concept: the streak page.** A designed habit-tracker page. A run of orange ✓s. Then one ✗.
Then today's empty box, and a pen deciding. The entire drama of the brand — the miss, the shame,
the choice — happens in one prop on one desk. The ✗ surrounded by ✓s is "never miss twice" as an
image: the miss becomes a scar in the streak, not the end of it.

**Why practical (phone), not AI-generated:** the retained lesson from intro v1 — *AI can't
reliably render handwritten text or precise hand choreography* — rules out generation for a
concept built on pen marks. This one is shot for real: phone, tripod, lamp. That's not a
compromise; real paper grain and real pen sound are the differentiator.

**Why hands only:** defers the face/semi-faceless decision without blocking anything, and
matches the ~70% semi-faceless base plan.

### Directions considered (for the record)
- **A. Stock-gym cut (v1/v2 plan).** Proven, fast, assets exist. Rejected as flagship: fails
  the screenshot test. Survives as *fallback* — VO + frames are done, could ship in an hour.
- **B. Desk-world streak page. ← CHOSEN.** Ownable, on-lesson (practical), props ≈ $0,
  and the prop itself becomes a future asset (see below).
- **C. AI-generated in the desk world (Higgsfield, like the intro shots).** Rejected: the
  concept centers on handwriting — the known AI failure mode. Also costs credits for something
  a phone does better here.

## Sound direction (the secret weapon)

Every motivation page runs a beat-drop. We run **foley**: pen scratch, paper slide, the lamp
click, chair creak — close and tactile (ASMR-adjacent), with only a low pulse under the back
half. The music→silence cut at 0:02 still happens (that's the proven scroll-stopper); what's
different is what fills the silence: *real sounds of someone getting back to work.*

**Voice: the founder's real voice.** Close-mic (phone under a blanket works), deadpan on
"Good." — warming slightly by "run it back." 3+ takes: deadpan / warm / mix, pick by ear.
`vo-adam.mp3` (AI) stays as fallback only. Imperfect-but-real is on-brand; polished-but-fake
is the competition.

## The prop is a product seed

The tracker page must be a **designed brand asset**, not a random notebook grid — brand type,
the ↻ mark, Blaze-orange ✓s on bone paper. Design it once as a print-ready file and it becomes:
1. the hero prop for this video (print 3–4 copies for takes),
2. the future lead-magnet tracker (Phase 2 "Reset Protocol") — the video is literally
   advertising the artifact before it exists,
3. a recurring set piece for future Today's Rep / Never Miss Twice posts.

→ Next asset to produce: `tracker-page.svg` (A5/A4 print file) in this folder.

## Practical shoot notes (phone-only)

- Lamp from the left, overheads off, curtains closed — protect the navy shadows. Desk lamp +
  one bounce (white paper) is enough.
- Tripod or book-stack; lock focus/exposure (tap-hold) before each take; 4K if available,
  30fps; wipe the lens.
- Shoot each shot 3–5×; macro shots: keep the phone ≥15cm back and crop in post (closer =
  focus hunting).
- Mark the pen's start position with tape for repeatable takes.
- Hands: no logos on sleeves; a plain watch is fine (on-brand, maker-adjacent).
- Do NOT reuse the intro video's crumple/dominoes actions here — same world, different props,
  so the two videos rhyme instead of repeating.
