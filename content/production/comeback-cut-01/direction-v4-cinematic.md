## 1. Anatomy of the reference (measured, not guessed)

**Genre:** cinematic identity edit — real footage of ONE person living the message (running,
desk, gym, night work), word-synced editorial typography, soft-spoken VO, film grade. The
creator IS the proof; the text is the thesis.

**Script (decoded from frames):**
> "Every time you hesitate and wonder, someone with half your ability and twice your hunger
> takes your spot. In this game, talent isn't rewarded. Consistency is… **Consistency wins.**"

**Structure (4 movements, ~14s):**
1. **The sentence (0–7.8s):** fast montage, ~14 cuts (0.3–0.7s each), one/two words landing
   per cut, synced to a soft VO. Shots alternate wide ↔ macro (run → eye close-up → charger
   plug → desk → shoes → gym) so the eye never settles.
2. **The pause (7.8–11.3s):** HARD pattern break — pure black screens, serif text only
   ("In this game, / talent isn't / rewarded. / Consistency is…"), music dips, ~3.5s. This is
   the retention trick: total silence mid-video makes you lean in.
3. **The hit (11.3–12.6s):** 2-frame WHITE flash (inverted: black text on white) —
   "Consistency wins." — then a micro-cut flurry back into footage.
4. **The exhale (12.6–14s):** one held shot (night desk, laptop glow), phrase stays on
   screen, fade out. (Video also OPENS on a 2-frame inverted-negative flash of the run shot —
   stylistic slap to stop the scroll.)

**Typography system (two tiers):**
- Connector words — small, light sans-serif, lowercase ("and", "you", "with", "takes your").
- Power words — 3–4× larger, elegant SERIF ITALIC ("time", "wonder", "someone", "ability",
  "twice", "hunger", "spot.", "game.", "rewarded.", "Consistency").
- Placed near the subject (not centered-bottom), one/two words per cut, each word *stays*
  briefly as the next appears. White throughout.

**Audio:** soft close-mic VO (intimate, almost under the breath — the opposite of the
"motivation announcer") + ambient/emotional track. Music ducks at movement 2, hits at the
white flash. Spectrogram confirms speech throughout with a gap exactly at the black section.

**Grade:** warm highlights (lamps, skin) / teal-navy shadows, slightly lifted blacks, film
grain, mild bloom on practicals, all-black wardrobe. Cohesion comes from the grade — the
locations vary, the color doesn't.

**Why it works:** motion density (cut rate) buys the first 3s → curiosity (the sentence is
incomplete until 8s) buys the middle → the black pause + flash is a dopamine spike → the
person doing real things makes the words credible. It's a *proof-of-life ad for a person*.

## 2. Run it Back adaptation — script

The reference's engine is a threat ("someone takes your spot") — that's grindset, off-brand.
We keep the STRUCTURE and flip the engine to ours: the fall isn't the enemy; staying down is.

**Script v4 (primary):**
> "Every time you miss once and let it become twice, you're not losing a streak —
> you're training the quit. In this game, perfect isn't rewarded. Coming back is…
> **NEVER MISS TWICE.** ↻ run it back"

- Mirrors the reference beat-for-beat (even lands "twice" where theirs lands "twice").
- "Training the quit" is the stakes-line: true (habit science), zero shame.
- The white-flash hit is the brand rule; the held outro is the sign-off + @handle.

**Alt (if primary feels harsh on the ear):**
> "Everybody falls off. But every day you stay down, the comeback gets one day heavier.
> In this game, perfect isn't rewarded. Showing back up is… **NEVER MISS TWICE.** ↻ run it back"

**Word map (primary) — type tier per beat:**

| 2 | you miss | *once* |

## 3. Shot list (12 clips, phone-only, ~2–3h across 3 setups)

All-black/dark-neutral wardrobe. Each clip: shoot 5–10s, use 0.3–0.8s. Alternate WIDE ↔ MACRO
in the edit. ✋ = hands/back/silhouette only; 👤 = face visible (optional — swap for ✋ takes
if staying semi-faceless; the reference itself shows face in only ~3 of 12 shots).

**Setup A — outside (golden hour if possible):**
1. ✋ Back view, running/walking away down a road — shoot from ~15m with 2–3× zoom
   (telephoto compression = the cinematic look). **Also the opening inverted-flash frame.**
2. ✋ Shoes hitting pavement, phone near ground, low angle.
3. 👤 Running toward camera, wide, landscape/street behind (far enough that face is small).
4. ✋ Pass-by blur: run past the camera close (whip of motion for a "twice"/transition beat).

**Setup B — desk (the existing desk world: walnut, lamp, black notebook):**
5. 👤/✋ Lean back from the screen, hands behind head, lamp warm, screen glow.
6. ✋ MACRO: pen strikes a ✓ on the **streak/tracker page** (v3's prop — lands on beat 3
   "twice"; the ✗→✓ page IS never-miss-twice in one image).
7. ✋ MACRO: keyboard typing, shallow focus.
8. ✋ Lamp click ON (light snaps on — good for a cut accent).
9. 👤 EYE macro: extreme close-up, blink (phone macro mode, window light). Face-safe — it's
   just an eye.
10. ✋ Crumpled page smoothed flat on the desk (rhymes with the intro video's language).

**Setup C — night/low light:**
11. 👤 Window silhouette, chin on hand, thinking (backlit = face in shadow, semi-faceless).
12. 👤/✋ Night desk, laptop glow only, profile — **the held closing shot.**

**Phone settings:** 4K 30 (60 for shots 2/4), lock focus+exposure (tap-hold), expose slightly
under, wipe lens, tripod/book-stack for B and C. Macro: stay ≥15cm back, crop in post.

## 4. Edit timeline (~15s target)

| Time | Movement | Content | Audio |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:00.1 | Slap | 2-frame inverted flash of shot 1 | music already playing |
| 0:00–0:08 | The sentence | beats 1–5, ~12–14 cuts, one beat ≈ 1–2 shots; word lands ON the cut | soft VO over ambient track |
| 0:08–0:11.5 | The pause | pure black, beats 6–8, serif only, text fades in word-group at a time | music ducks ~70%; VO drops to near-whisper on "Coming back is…" |
| 0:11.5–0:12 | The hit | WHITE frame, "NEVER MISS TWICE." black/Blaze text + 3–4 micro-cuts (shots 6, 2, 4) | music hit / drum on the flash |
| 0:12–0:15 | The exhale | held shot 12, "↻ run it back" + @handle fades in, fade to black | music resolves, tail reverb |

**Loop:** fade-to-black end → inverted-flash open reads as a seamless restart.
**Cover frame:** shot 1 back-view with "Every *time*" type — legible at grid size.

## 5. Type & grade spec (CapCut)

- **Sans (connectors + the rule card):** Inter / HarmonyOS Sans (CapCut default ok), light,
  white, ~40% size of serif.
- **Serif italic (power words):** Playfair Display Italic (free, Google Fonts — import into
  CapCut). White; Blaze `#FF4A1C` reserved ONLY for "NEVER MISS TWICE." and the ↻ mark.
- Words appear with a 2–3 frame fade/scale-in (102→100%), placed in negative space near the
  subject, NOT bottom-center. Previous word may linger one beat.
- **Grade (manual, all clips identically):** temperature +4…+6 · saturation −10 · contrast +8
  · highlights −10 · shadows +5 tinted teal (HSL: shadows→blue-cyan) · blacks slightly lifted
  · grain 20–30 · subtle vignette. Or one "film" preset at ~65% strength on everything.

## 6. VO direction

Founder's real voice. **Soft, close, intimate — spoken to one person at 1am, not announced.**
This is a different voice from Template A's "Good." confrontation — don't mix them.
Record: quiet room, phone 10–15cm from mouth, under a duvet if echoey; 3 takes (soft /
softer / almost-whisper); pick by ear. Total read ≈ 11–12s leaving air at both ends.

## 7. Music

Emotional ambient with a builds-then-resolves arc (search CapCut/platform audio: "cinematic
ambient emotional", "aesthetic vlog piano"; prefer a trending on-platform sound in that mood —
layer it LOW, VO leads). Needs: a duckable mid-section and one hit-point for the flash.

## 8. What carries over / what this replaces

- **Replaces:** v1 stock-gym edit and v3 single-prop desk drama as the flagship.
- **Carries over:** never-miss-twice as the one idea · the desk world as Setup B · the streak
  page prop (shot 6) — still worth designing as a print file (doubles as the lead magnet) ·
  founder VO · loop thinking · caption from v2 (unchanged).
- **Face decision:** this format is person-led but face-light (3 of 12 shots, all swappable
  to silhouette/back). Shooting both variants of shots 3/5/12 costs 10 extra minutes and
  keeps the choice open until the edit.
